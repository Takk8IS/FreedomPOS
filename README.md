Freedom POS is a modern and intuitive point of sale (POS) system, developed to transform the way you manage your business. With an elegant interface and powerful features, Freedom POS offers a complete solution for sales management, inventory, and customer relationship.

## ✨ Why choose Freedom POS?

- 🎯 **Modern Interface** - Intuitive and responsive design
- 🚀 **High Performance** - Fast and efficient even with large volumes of data
- 💻 **Cross-platform** - Works on Windows, macOS, and Linux
- 🔒 **Security** - Advanced protection for your data
- ☁️ **Automatic Backup** - Your data is always secure
- 🌙 **Dark Mode** - Adaptable interface for your comfort

## ✨ Features

- 📊 **Dashboard** - Business overview with important metrics
- 💰 **Sales** - Complete management of sales and transactions
- 📦 **Products** - Product registration and management
- 👥 **Customers** - Customer management and history
- 📈 **Reports** - Detailed reports and analysis
- ⚙️ **Settings** - System customization
- 👤 **Profile** - User and permission management

## 💼 System Requirements

- Windows 10/11, macOS 10.15+ or Linux
- 4GB RAM (8GB recommended)
- 500MB disk space
